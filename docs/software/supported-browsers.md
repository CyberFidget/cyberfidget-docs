# Supported browsers

After this page you know which features work in your browser and when you need a computer and a cable.

## What works in any up-to-date browser

- Writing code and drawing art in the studio.
- Trying apps in the emulator, a copy of the device that runs in your browser.
- Using the code generator on the **Generate** tab.
- Sharing your work and browsing The Archives.
- Sending app changes over WiFi to a [linked Fidget](link-your-fidget.md).

## What needs a computer and a cable

Connecting with a cable works from a computer with Chrome, Edge or Firefox. These features need a Universal Serial Bus (USB) connection from the browser:

- Installing firmware, the device's built-in software, from the [website update page](https://cyberfidget.com/update/). This includes **Erase everything and reinstall**.
- Using **Send to my Fidget** over USB in the studio.
- First-time setup by cable on My Fidget.
- Setting up WiFi over USB.

Connecting your Cyber Fidget over USB from an Android phone may not work, even when the browser says it can. On a phone, use the [WiFi route](#if-usb-isnt-available) instead.

## If USB isn't available

If your browser can't connect over USB, the page tells you and offers the WiFi route. The update page hides the install steps and disables **Install update**.

On the Fidget, open **Settings > Setup WiFi**. Then [link your Fidget](https://cyberfidget.com/my-fidget/link/) to your account. [Link your Fidget](link-your-fidget.md) walks through the steps. App changes arrive over WiFi at its next check-in; see [Updates](updates.md).

Installing firmware from the website needs the cable.
