# Supported models

After this page you know where to find the models the studio can use to write code from your description.

## Three ways to run the generator

The **App Builder** card on [My account](https://cyberfidget.com/my-fidget/account/) offers **Free**, **Use my own key** and, for accounts that have credits, **Use my credits**. The card works without signing in; signing in adds your profile and credits. See [Free use, credits and privacy](credits.md) for costs, limits and setup.

Free generations use the model Cyber Fidget picks. For credit generations, choose one in **Model for credit generations** in the **App Builder** card.

## Use your own key

An API key is a secret code that lets the studio use your account with a provider. In **My account**'s **App Builder** card, choose **Use my own key**. The **Provider** choices are **Anthropic**, **OpenAI** and **Google Gemini (free!)**.

Below **API Key** and its **Save**, **Cancel** and **Verify** buttons, the card links to the selected provider through **Get an Anthropic key**, **Get an OpenAI key** or **Get a free Gemini key**. The **Model** field follows; **Refresh model list** asks the provider which models your key can use. The **Max output tokens** slider limits the length of a reply, measured in small pieces of text called tokens.

See [Choosing a model](ota-builder.md#choosing-a-model) for where to choose in **My account**.
