## Color filters

<div class="cf-row" markdown>
<div class="cf-row__media" markdown>

![Three Cyber Fidgets in a row, their screens glowing through dark, red and teal color filters](../assets/product/screen-filters.webp){ loading=lazy width="1000" height="1000" }

</div>
<div class="cf-row__text" markdown>

A thin color filter sits in the case front, over the screen window, and changes the color the screen glows. You pick one when you build your Cyber Fidget.

Available colors include:

- **Red** -- warm, saturated glow
- **Teal** -- cool accent tone
- **Blue** -- cool accent tone

The filter goes in before the mainboard ([Assembly step 5](../getting-started/assembly/guide.md#step-5-install-color-filter)), so changing it later means opening the case and lifting the mainboard out.

</div>
</div>

## 3D printing

Enclosure models are available for 3D printing, so you can design and print your own backplates, cases, or modifications.

!!! tip "Design your own"
    The aluminum chassis dimensions are documented in the [Hardware Specs](../hardware/specs.md). Use these as reference when designing custom parts. STL files are also planned to be made available.
